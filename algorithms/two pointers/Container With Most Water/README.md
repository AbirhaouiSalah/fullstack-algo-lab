# Container With Most Water

## Problem Statement

Given non-negative heights, choose two lines that form a container holding the
maximum amount of water. The area is the distance between the lines multiplied
by the shorter height.

## Hints

- Hint 1: begin with the widest container and move the shorter boundary inward.
- Hint 2: track the best area while shrinking the interval.
